Enquiring about a software or AI project

The first conversation is free and there is no obligation attached to it. It helps if you arrive with three things: what the product is meant to do, who is going to use it, and roughly what you have to spend. You do not need a specification document: most clients do not have one, and writing it is part of what we do next.

What you get back is a written scope with a screen list, a phased plan and a fixed price per phase, usually within a few working days for a normal-sized build. Nothing starts until you have agreed that document. If your idea is better served by an off-the-shelf product than by custom software, we will tell you that instead of selling you a build.

We work with clients across Pakistan and abroad: remote delivery is normal for us, with recorded demos and asynchronous updates for clients in other time zones. Payment is by cash, bank transfer, Easypaisa, JazzCash or international wire, in PKR or USD.

Asking about course admissions

Our courses run on-site here in Kohat and live online, and admission is open to everyone: students, working professionals, and people changing career, with no restriction on background or gender. If you are unsure which course fits, call or message on WhatsApp and describe what you want to be doing in a year; that is a more useful starting point than a course title.

Worth asking about when you get in touch: current fee and duration, class timings and whether an evening batch is running, whether the next batch is on-site or online, what laptop or hardware you need, and what you finish the course with. We teach in English, Urdu and Pashto, so language is not a barrier to enrolling.
