Internships at a working software house

Infusible Coder Pvt Ltd is a software house and IT training center on Near Fubs CNG / Pizza Hut, Pindi Road, Kohat. We take interns into the same projects our paid team works on: not a separate track of exercises invented to keep interns busy.

What an intern actually does here

You are given real tickets on a real codebase, starting small and getting larger as you show what you can handle. Your work goes through the same code review as everyone else's, which means it gets rejected sometimes. That is the part you cannot get from a course.

  • Working in a shared repository with branches, pull requests and code review
  • Reading an existing codebase you did not write. The skill nobody teaches
  • Building features against a written scope, not a tutorial
  • Testing on real low-end devices, because that is what our users have
  • Sitting in on client demos so you see how requirements actually arrive

Areas you can intern in

Web development

React front ends, Node.js and NestJS services, PostgreSQL and MongoDB.

Mobile app development

Flutter and native Android and iOS, with the backend behind them.

AI and machine learning

Generative AI assistants, retrieval systems, NLP and computer vision.

UI/UX design

User flows, wireframes, design systems and prototypes in Figma.

Who we take

University students needing a required internship, recent graduates, and people who have taught themselves and want to work on something real. A degree is not a requirement: being able to show us something you have built matters more than where you studied. Everyone is welcome to apply regardless of background or gender.

What we look for: you can already write code in at least one language, you can explain something you have built, and you are prepared to have your work reviewed. Everything else is teachable.

How to apply

Send us three things. A short note on what you want to work on, anything you have built (a GitHub link, an app, a design file, a college project), and when you are available and for how long.

Email infusiblecoder@gmail.com or message +92-335-5885039 on WhatsApp. If your university requires a formal letter, tell us which format and we will issue it. We reply within one working day; we are closed Friday.

If you are searching for an internship near you and you are in Kohat, Peshawar, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Hangu or anywhere else in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, we are on Near Fubs CNG / Pizza Hut, Pindi Road in Kohat: walk in during working hours (Saturday to Thursday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (closed Friday)) or apply remotely.

Common questions

Is the internship paid?

Ask when you apply. It depends on the track, the duration and what you can already do. We will give you a straight answer before you commit any time, rather than leaving it vague.

Can I do it remotely?

Yes for most tracks. On-site in Kohat gets you more, simply because you overhear the conversations that never make it into a ticket, but remote works and plenty of our own delivery is remote.

How long does it run?

It depends on what your university requires and what you want out of it. Tell us the window you have and we will tell you honestly what is achievable in it.

Will I get a certificate or letter?

Yes. A completion certificate, and any specific format your university needs.

Do I need to take a paid course first?

No. The internship and the courses are separate. If a course would genuinely close a gap we will say so, but it is not a condition of applying.

Could it turn into a job?

It has done. We hire from people whose work we already know, so an internship is the most direct route onto the team, but we are not going to promise a role that depends on whether we are hiring at the time.
