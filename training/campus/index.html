Find the Infusible Coder center in Kohat

The center is listed near Fubs CNG and Pizza Hut on Pindi Road, Kohat, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Use the map link provided on this page for directions rather than relying on a copied address from an unofficial directory. The contact details are shared from the site's central business information.

Published business hours are Saturday to Thursday, from morning to late afternoon, with Friday closed. Contact the center before travelling to confirm that someone relevant to your course enquiry will be available, particularly when you want to discuss a current batch or admission question.

What to ask before a visit

Tell the team which course area you want to discuss and ask whether a visit is the best next step. Confirm the current schedule, the person or desk handling enquiries, and any information you should bring. This prevents a general campus visit from being mistaken for a confirmed class appointment.

If you have mobility, communication, or other access needs, contact the center before visiting so the team can explain the current arrangements. This page does not make assumptions about facilities that have not been documented; a direct conversation is the reliable way to plan.
